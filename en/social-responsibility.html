About the Project

Há Direito

Together with André Moreira Simões, I founded Há Direito with a clear purpose: to make the law more accessible, approachable and useful, regardless of each person’s socioeconomic background.

Through sessions in schools, universities and social housing communities, we sought to promote legal literacy and demonstrate that understanding the law is also a way of participating more consciously in society.

About the Project

3 Milhões de Nós

Speaking to more than one thousand young people at Aula Magna in Lisbon, I shared some of the experiences and lessons that shaped my roots.

More than telling my own story, I wanted to challenge them to question prejudice, move beyond labels and see each person beyond first impressions.

About the Project

UNICEF

At the invitation of UNICEF Portugal, I participated in the second Rights to the Future Conference, where, alongside several teachers, I highlighted the role of schools as drivers of social mobility.

Drawing on a legal analysis of the legislation governing TEIP schools, I advocated for more effective measures and action plans to reduce inequality and ensure genuine opportunities for all children and young people.

Financial Literacy

NOVA

About the Project

Financial Literacy

At the NOVA Financial Markets Knowledge Centre, a research centre at NOVA School of Law, I led the value creation area, where I designed initiatives and established partnerships with organisations in the financial sector to promote financial literacy among young people.

One of these initiatives helped children and young people living in a residential care home better understand how to manage their allowance, save money and make other everyday financial decisions.

Hotel Baía, Cascais

About the Project

Open Worlds

When I was challenged to create an initiative that would introduce young people to new professional and educational environments, I designed Open Worlds, a project I now coordinate.

Through supported experiences, mentoring and skills development, we seek to help them discover new interests, build confidence and recognise that different paths are possible for their future.
