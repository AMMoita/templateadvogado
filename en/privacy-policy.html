Privacy Policy

Transparency about how this website handles personal data and online technologies.

1. Who is responsible for processing?

This website presents the professional activity of Leonel Gomes Cá, a lawyer, and related initiatives. For data protection enquiries about this website, contact geral@nativelegal.pt or the professional address Av. da Liberdade 224, 2nd Floor, 1250-148 Lisbon, Portugal.

Enquiries addressed to Native Legal may be processed by the entity actually providing legal services. The formal identity of the controller and the allocation of responsibilities, where applicable, must be confirmed before the contact form is enabled.

2. What data may be processed?

When you browse this website, technical data necessary to provide and secure the pages may be processed, including IP address, date and time of access, browser details and server requests. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

If you contact us voluntarily through the stated professional channels, information that you provide may be processed, including your name, email address, telephone number, subject and message. Please avoid including sensitive personal data, confidential documents or legally privileged information in an initial, unsecured enquiry.

Contact form: in this version, the form displayed on the website is not connected to a sending service, and the information typed into it is not transmitted by the website. This policy will be reviewed before the form is activated to identify the service provider, the purposes and actual conditions of processing.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Provision, operation and security of the website: legitimate interests in delivering and protecting the service, without prejudice to the hosting provider's own processing.
  • Responding to professional enquiries by email, phone or other channels: pre-contractual steps, where applicable, or legitimate interests in responding.
  • Compliance with legal and professional obligations: compliance with a legal obligation, where applicable.
  • Audience measurement using Google Analytics: only with prior consent if this tool is enabled in the future. It is not active in V8.

The contact form statement “I have read and accept the Privacy Policy” is intended to confirm that the visitor has been shown this information; it does not replace the requirement to identify a lawful basis for each processing activity.

4. Providers and recipients

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. According to its documentation, the provider may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. The website loads DM Sans and Playfair Display through Google Fonts, generating technical requests to Google servers.

Google Analytics is planned but not enabled in this version. If enabled, this policy must be updated to reflect the configuration, recipients and applicable safeguards.

Information is only shared with technical and professional providers when necessary for the stated purposes or to comply with a legal obligation. Links to social networks and external platforms lead to services governed by their own privacy policies.

5. International transfers

Some digital service providers may process or access information outside the European Economic Area. Where relevant to processing under the website controller's responsibility, applicable GDPR transfer conditions and safeguards will be required. Specific transfer arrangements must be confirmed with service providers before adding further services.

6. Retention and security

Professional enquiry data is retained only for as long as necessary to respond, manage any subsequent professional relationship or comply with legal and professional obligations. Retention periods for technical hosting logs are set by the provider and should be checked in its privacy notice.

Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards are used in light of the services actually employed. No internet communication can be guaranteed entirely risk-free.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to certain processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

To exercise your rights or ask a question, contact geral@nativelegal.pt. You may also lodge a complaint with the Portuguese Data Protection Authority (CNPD).

8. Cookies, fonts and analytics

Local storage and any future analytics cookies are explained in our Cookies Policy. You can view and change preferences using “Cookie preferences” in the footer. Analytics preferences are separate from the acknowledgement on the contact form.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in features, providers, purposes or legal requirements. This version was prepared on 9 October 2026.
