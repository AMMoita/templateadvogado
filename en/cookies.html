Cookies Policy

Transparency about how this website handles personal data and online technologies.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files or identifiers stored in a browser or device. This website may also use equivalent technologies, such as browser localStorage. Not all these technologies require consent: this depends on their purpose.

3. Google Analytics — if enabled later

If Google Analytics is enabled in the future, we will load its code only after your explicit analytics consent. Refusing consent will not prevent you from using the website. V8 includes consent controls for this purpose, but tracking remains switched off until a valid, reviewed configuration is supplied.

A default GA4 configuration may use cookies such as _ga and _ga_<ID>, typically with a default lifespan of up to two years, depending on the final settings. The definitive list will be updated when analytics goes live.

4. Manage your preferences

When analytics is enabled, you will be able to accept, reject, customise and subsequently withdraw consent using the button below or the footer. Strictly necessary technologies and storage of your preference are not subject to the same consent requirement. You can also delete cookies through your browser settings.

5. Further information

For more on personal data and your rights, see our Privacy Policy. See Google's privacy information and GitHub's privacy notice.
